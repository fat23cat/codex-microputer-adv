# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Codex users who keep several tasks moving at once and want a dedicated,
glanceable hardware control surface beside the Mac. The primary interaction is
to notice a changed task and open it immediately with one numbered key.

## Product Purpose

Cardputer ADV behaves as a native Codex Micro over USB or Bluetooth. It mirrors
the six host task slots, their states, the selected task and Codex settings,
without inventing metadata the native protocol does not provide.

## Brand Personality

Precise, instrumental, calm. The physical immediacy and graphic confidence of
Teenage Engineering with the restraint and legibility expected from Apple.

## Anti-references

No dashboard chrome, explanatory labels, counters, fake timers, cyberpunk
decoration, ornamental focus frames or motion without a state-changing cause.

## Design Principles

- Show only state that comes from Codex.
- Make the six physical number keys and six screen slots one obvious system.
- A changed task may claim the display; routine refreshes remain invisible.
- Selection feedback must be immediate and independent from task status.
- Preserve control and readability through disconnected and burst-event states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

State is conveyed by slot position and number as well as colour. Text and digits
must remain legible at the 10% dim level. Motion is short except for the explicit
status-event hold, and input remains available while animation is running.
